      How the ledger calculates interest

      The rule can be read straight off the ledger rows.

      1. ChargeEach month the interest is one sixtieth (1.667%) of the Bill Arrear.
      2. AddThe closing balance includes that month's interest.
      3. CarryNext month's Bill Arrear equals this month's closing balance, so the interest now earns interest.
